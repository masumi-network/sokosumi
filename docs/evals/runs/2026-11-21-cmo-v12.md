## Why the failures happened

- **Strategy.** Both versions break the same strategy rules about as often: a side channel over three a week, a fifth video, two entries with one title, a phrase in three or more titles. cmo-v12 changes only the SEO skill, and no code reads the old skill's id. Some phrase failures come from the check itself: it counts "450 000" as the facts "450" and "000", and "f13" as a fact.
- **SEO cases on the first cmo-v12 run.** The new weekly-report cases connect a stand-in Search Console with cal.com's numbers on the lane, and it stayed for every later case. Cuso then answered "how do I rank for calendly alternative" from those numbers (position 12.6, 960 impressions) instead of asking the SEO specialist. Fixed in `cde3b1e632`: every Cuso chat case starts with it removed. All three pass 2/2 since.
- **`cmo-tiktok-waits-for-owner`.** The router picks DELEGATE_TASK or CLARIFY on both versions; this case fails on cmo-v9 too.
- **SEO cases on cmo-v10.** cmo-v10's stronger model answers short and leaves the detail to the chat card: the refused idea is explained in the card, not the reply, so the judge fails it. On a check that timed out it sometimes answers without reading the SEO page first. Both happen on cmo-v10 alone. cmo-v12 adds a rule to name a refused idea and why; the reply now mentions it, but still points to the card.
- **`cmo-ads-yes`, `cmo-competitor-second-hand`.** One-off misses: both pass 2/2 on rerun.

## Verdict

Ship cmo-v12 as a version. It is no worse than cmo-v9 on the CMO suite or cmo-v10 on the SEO cases, and adds the weekly SEO report, page changes as before and after, and articles on request. Keep it off the hire default and move no Cuso until Patrick says so.

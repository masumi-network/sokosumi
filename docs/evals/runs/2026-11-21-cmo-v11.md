## Failed cases

### `cmo-month-picture-variety`: cmo-v11 on `4db1fe66fd`

- Failed check: Month made: never reached; the case was still waiting after 3 hours and was cancelled
- Jev read the month: no review

## Why the failures happened

- **The month never reached its review.** One piece's five alternatives and its GPT Image remake were all rejected. `cmoMediaState` does not read the pick, so that slot read as "making" for ever instead of "failed". Production never ended, and the review never started. Fixed: a remake starts only after a pick, so a remake now stands in for the pick record, and the slot fails.
- **Every alternative drew a date and a price.** The layout description said "a month at top left, a price at top right", and all five alternatives drew "OCT 2024" and a price. The ask now names text only by its size and place. The description is also cut at a whole sentence instead of mid-word. In the final run, 1 of 33 rejections was for a date or price; in the real month it was 45 of 189.

## Verdict

Ship cmo-v11 as a version. It builds on cmo-v10 (#7830), which must merge first. Keep cmo-v11 off the hire default and move no Cuso until Patrick has seen the month sheet. A chosen picture costs about three times as much as on cmo-v9 ($0.20 against $0.07), and the extra pays for the five alternatives and the side-by-side pick.

## Why the failures happened

None comes from routing. The Sol turns fail only where every model fails (cmo-ads-yes) or where the database copy does not deliver Core's own message. The Luna turns fail by chance (they pass on repeat) or in the same way on cmo-v8 and cmo-v9 in the same copy.

## Verdict

Ship cmo-v10: strategy goes from 7 of 11 to 16 of 16 across every strategy case, and nothing regresses that routing touches.

## Why the failures happened

v37 changes one skill (inbox, calendar and X messages), the X DM tool gate, and the ingest/stand-up packets. None of the regressed or failing cases touch them; all four X DM cases pass.

- `mcp-read-issues`, `mcp-uses-server-not-web`, `mcp-ignores-injected-orders`, `a2a-hop-limit`, `chat-identity-from-brief`: the bot had the right answer in hand; Core replaced it with `HELD_BACK_REPLY` ("I held back this reply because it could not be checked just now", `action-response.ts`), which Core shows when the claim check cannot run. That is the preview's Jev call failing, not the version.
- `coworker-failure`, `hand-over-on-completion`, `archive-dropped-task`, `seo-save-page-group`: a tool failed on the preview (`reply_to_task`, `assign_task`, `preview_result`), not a choice the bot made.
- `remember-standing-rule`, `announcement-not-for-every-bot`, `break-task-into-subtasks`, `a2a-who-to-ask`, `mcp-write-asked`: single-repeat judge or route misses in areas v37 does not change; the other recent preview runs on this day scored low too (#8228 cmo 2/4 and 3/7, #8219 cmo 20/27).
- The 7 already-failing cases failed in the weekly run as well.
- `x-dm-bulk` first failed on wording ("Nothing to add." instead of "Nothing new worth flagging."); in production a bulk-only check never wakes the bot. The case now accepts either quiet answer and passes.

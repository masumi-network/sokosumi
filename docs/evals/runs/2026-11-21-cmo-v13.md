## What cmo-v13 is

cmo-v11 (cmo-v10's stronger model for hard work, pictures on a print's layout picked from five alternatives) with two skills swapped:

| Skill    | cmo-v11               | cmo-v13                     |
| -------- | --------------------- | --------------------------- |
| SEO      | `cmo-seo`             | `cmo-seo-reports` (cmo-v12) |
| Strategy | `cmo-strategy-claims` | `cmo-strategy-part-patch`   |

`cmo-strategy-part-patch` is cmo-v8's claims strategy with one rule changed: every change the owner asks for to part of the plan is a part patch, never the whole calendar, and he never asks the owner for the calendar. Core's change message says the same for cmo-v13 (NMKR, 2026-11-21: told to resend the whole 160-entry calendar, Cuso asked the owner for "the full strategy calendar").

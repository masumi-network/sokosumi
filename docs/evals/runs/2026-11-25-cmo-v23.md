## Failed cases

Fail on both versions with the same checks, as listed in the cmo-v22 run: the month production cases that need pictures, the competitor messages (Ably 401 locally), the SEO saves (no keyword research locally), `cmo-research-utxo-ag`, `cmo-ads-yes`, `cmo-tiktok-waits-for-owner`.

The 9 that passed on cmo-v22 and failed here, with the reruns:

- `cmo-month-calendar-nmkr-io-active`, `cmo-month-calendar-entry-fyi-all-in`: Core's month writer with frozen plans; no Cuso turn and no context. Repeated points and one post short; 2 of 2 and 1 of 2 on cmo-v23, 1 of 2 and 1 of 2 on cmo-v22.
- `cmo-strategy-website-only`: a strategy turn sees the full fitted context on both versions. "New:" missing on networks the shared eval Project had connected from earlier cases (leftover connections); cmo-v22 failed the same way on a rerun.
- `cmo-contact-list`: routed DELEGATE_TASK by the classifier, which runs before the organizer; 2 of 2 on both after.
- `cmo-no-invented-numbers`, `cmo-seo-slow-check`: judge and number checks; 2 of 2 on cmo-v23 after.
- `cmo-change-reply-plain`, `cmo-reply-language-follow-up`: fail every rerun on both versions (asks instead of saving; answers "Nothing to add.").
- `cmo-competitor-quiet-week`: writes a sentence instead of "Nothing to add."; 1 of 2 on both.

## Why the failures happened

None traced to the organized context: in each failed turn the facts the judge wanted were in the picked part or read with a read tool. The differences between versions are within run-to-run noise on this setup.

## Verdict

cmo-v23 is as good as cmo-v22 on the suite, with 4 new cases that only it passes. Not the hire default (that is cmo-v20, and cmo-v22 waits for the owner too); no live Cuso moves until the owner says so.

# Setup: later changes

**Steps (any of these, any time)**

- Adds or removes a channel in Channels.
- Reads the CMO Terms, Privacy Policy, DPA and Imprint beside the subscription in Settings.
- Changes the plan, tops up credits, opens billing.
- Changes the time zone.
- Hires a second Cuso for another website, and switches between them.
- Reads the AI content notice and can add cmo.xyz as a preferred Google source in Settings.
- Starts over (archives this Cuso).
- Asks Cuso in chat for a change to the month.

**They expect**

- Each change takes effect without breaking the month.
- Money changes are confirmed before they happen.
- Start over says exactly what ends and what stays.
- A change asked in chat comes back as Cuso's card saying what he did; his answer adds only what the card leaves out, in their words, never his tools, saves or checks (cmo-v6).

# X every day

**Who:** a founder who wants a steady presence on X without living on it.

**Steps**

1. Connects X.
2. Lets Cuso post about three times a day from the month's themes; about one in three has a picture.
3. Mostly doesn't look. Now and then opens the Calendar to see what's coming.
4. When something happens ("we shipped dark mode", "jump on this news"), says so in chat; Cuso drafts a post, shows it in the chat, and the owner approves it.

**They expect**

- Short, sharp posts that fit X, not shortened LinkedIn posts.
- No two posts saying the same thing, no reused pictures.
- A post asked for in chat goes out within minutes of approving it, and can still be stopped.

# Month review

**Who:** every owner, once a month.

**Steps**

1. At month end Cuso writes in chat: what went out, the numbers per channel (and from Analytics, if connected), what worked, what didn't.
2. He proposes next month: more of what worked, less of what didn't, any change of pace or budget.
3. The owner changes what they disagree with in chat ("more LinkedIn, drop X") and approves.
4. Next month's pieces appear on the Calendar.

**They expect**

- One clear answer to "is this working?", with real numbers.
- A short decision, not a report to study.
- Their changes show up in the new month.

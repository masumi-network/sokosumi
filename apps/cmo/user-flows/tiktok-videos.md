# TikTok videos

**Who:** a consumer brand that knows its customers are on TikTok but has no one to film.

**Steps**

1. Connects TikTok.
2. Asks in chat for a TikTok, or lets the month's plan include them.
3. Cuso writes the script and makes a presenter video: hook in the first second, captions in the safe area, music, B-roll of the product or site, an end card.
4. Watches it in chat, asks for changes or approves it.
5. It posts to TikTok, marked as AI-made, and the same video goes to Reels and Shorts.

**They expect**

- It looks native to TikTok, not like an ad.
- They never have to film or edit.
- The AI label is set for them.

**Today:** posting to TikTok works only for utxo until TikTok approves our app audit (#7443). The video is made and goes to Reels and Shorts.

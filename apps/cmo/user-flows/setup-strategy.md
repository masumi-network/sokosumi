# Setup: Strategy

**Steps**

1. Reads the strategy while it's written: the one-sentence idea, the funnel (Awareness, Interest, Desire, Action, drawn once at its own proportions beside the stages) each step with its plain goal and about what share of the month's posts serves it (no networks there: every network helps at every step), a phone for every network the month goes out on ("Where it shows up", TikTok and YouTube included, counted in each network's own words: Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikToks), "What the posts do" (how many helpful tips, product posts, customer proof and offers, each with one real example) with the topics in one plain line under it ("Topics Cuso keeps coming back to: … · … · …", no shares, no networks), the month day by day. From cmo-v20 each pillar is something the buyer gets or does, never a reassurance line the site repeats, and rests on at least three Brand Brain facts: Core's review names one that doesn't and Cuso merges or reframes it, so a simple product can have three pillars. From cmo-v21 the topics come from Core's research into the buyers' industry (news, trends, common problems, competitors, each with its source, under Brand research/Industry), never steps of using the product: most posts help buyers with their own work and the product comes in where it helps.
2. Sees every network CMO offers under "Where Cuso works" (X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok), also the ones they have no account on, marked "New"; a network Cuso left out is named with his reason.
3. Asks for a change in words if they want one, or, under Ask for a change, has Cuso write a whole new strategy ("Write a new strategy", the same fresh request as a new hire's). There is no pace to pick: the month is written at the most a plan makes and reads "Up to N posts" until they have a plan; the plan they buy next decides how much of it is made.
4. Answers "Do you want ads?" with Yes or No. No is the start; after Yes they set a monthly budget, which is extra to their plan, and Cuso plans the ads around it. A budget set while Cuso is still writing or changing the strategy waits, and he plans it as soon as he is done.
5. Says no to an assumption chip if it's wrong; Cuso updates the strategy.
6. Optionally connects measurement accounts in "How we know it works" (Results). If they cancel OAuth, they return to Results and see the error once; leaving Results (to another tab or step) and coming back shows no error.
7. Sees what each plan makes per network (Starter, Growth, Scale) and approves. A strategy can always be approved: Cuso gets short turns to fill what is missing (six on cmo-v14 and later). Each turn names the two shortest weeks, what every network still lacks in them and the titles they already hold, so Cuso never needs the whole calendar. Only when those turns are spent does Core fit the strategy to what its calendar holds: every network's cadence (pictures and Reels apart) and its funnel plays, and a "Less than Cuso planned" card lists each cut. Once approved, the step still offers "Write a new strategy" until the first plan is bought: the approved one stays until the new one is approved, then its unsent posts are canceled.

**They expect**

- A month they could picture: how many posts, where, about what, how many videos.
- The month they approve holds as many posts as its own pace and channel cadences say, and every video its funnel promises.
- Changes reflected without starting over.
- A "No" or a correction reaches the whole strategy: the goal, the funnel, the questions, the ads line and every post title that rested on it, and the Brand Brain's summary, audience and offers say the corrected thing too. Nothing still names what they said no to (cmo-v8).
- Each network gets what suits it (cmo-v14): several X posts a day, at most one a day on LinkedIn, mostly pictures, TikToks, Reels and YouTube Shorts every day, every format by its platform's name; each funnel step says in plain words what happens for the business (Awareness: people find out about you; Interest: they look at what you offer; Desire: they want it; Action: they sign up, buy or book); every network helps at every step, leaning toward what it does best (cmo-v20 tags each post with its step); a connected network is never left out.
- Helpful tips about their buyers' world, not product tutorials in disguise, and every fact about the market with its source (cmo-v21).
- Every post makes its own point: no two posts in a week argue the same thing, and no point comes back more than three times in the month (cmo-v8).
- To understand what they will have to do themselves.
- To understand every line without being a marketer: plain words, no jargon (impressions, leads, funnel, cadence), and the same numbers on every screen (cmo-v15).
- No ads, no ad budget and no question about ads unless they said yes.
- To see what Cuso does for their website (the weekly audit, page fixes, articles when they ask), though it is not on the calendar, and to find it in the funnel.
- Questions that check an assumption with a yes or no, never whether they can count or share something.
- Posts planned everywhere their buyers could be, not only where they already have an account; the ones for a network they have not connected wait finished on the Calendar.
- Cancelling a measurement OAuth connection shows the error in Results, and leaving Results and returning shows no error.

**Missing today:** TikTok is planned only once it is open for posting. A Cuso hired before cmo-v5 still plans only the networks the business has an account on.

# Setup: Strategy

**Steps**

1. Reads the strategy while it's written: the goal as the headline with three short facts under it (Who, The offer, Where as network marks; no longer sentence). The Strategy page shows no post counts anywhere (owner, 2026-11-23): no "Up to N posts", no per-plan table, no count on the month or its calendar button; how much each plan makes is on Subscribe, the funnel (Awareness, Interest, Desire, Action, drawn once at its own proportions beside the stages) each step with its plain goal and about what share of the month's posts serves it (no networks there: every network helps at every step), a phone for every network the month goes out on ("Where it shows up", TikTok and YouTube included, named in each network's own words: Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikToks, never counted), "What the posts do" (helpful tips, product posts, customer proof and offers, each a share bar with one real example, no counts) with the topics in one plain line under it ("Topics Cuso keeps coming back to: … · … · …", no shares, no networks), the month day by day ("Your month": a short video going out on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok is one card with the three network marks; each day shows three cards and "+N more" opens the rest; cards carry no topic chip). From cmo-v20 each pillar is something the buyer gets or does, never a reassurance line the site repeats, and rests on at least three Brand Brain facts: Core's review names one that doesn't and Cuso merges or reframes it, so a simple product can have three pillars. From cmo-v21 the topics come from Core's research into the buyers' industry (news, trends, common problems, competitors, each with its source, under Brand research/Industry), never steps of using the product: most posts help buyers with their own work and the product comes in where it helps. From cmo-v22 "Your month" holds only 3 to 5 example posts, each on a different topic and network, one of them a short video, with the note "These are example posts. Once you subscribe, I plan every post your plan makes, topic by topic, and keep these in it." Funnel steps show no shares until the month is planned.
2. Sees every network CMO offers under "Where Cuso works" (X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok), also the ones they have no account on, marked "New", each card naming what Cuso makes there (no counts or per-day rates, no "Images/Videos by Cuso" tags), plus the Newsletter, "Your website and Google search", and the ad network Cuso suggests ("Ads, only if you say yes" until they do); a network Cuso left out is named with his reason.
3. Asks for a change in words if they want one, or, under Ask for a change, has Cuso write a whole new strategy ("Write a new strategy", the same fresh request as a new hire's). There is no pace to pick: the month is written at the most a plan makes; the plan they buy next decides how much of it is made.
4. Answers "Do you want ads?" with Yes or No. Until they answer neither is picked, and Cuso's proposal is said in words ("Cuso suggests CHF 600 a month on Google Ads. …"). After Yes they set a monthly budget (Cuso's amount filled in), which is extra to their plan, and Cuso plans the ads around it. No, when the plan has no ads anyway, is only kept: no re-plan, no new version. A budget set while Cuso is still writing or changing the strategy waits, and he plans it as soon as he is done. The question stays on the strategy after approval and on the app's Strategy page (owners and admins): a new budget there becomes a new version of the strategy, and the ads start once they approve it.
5. Says no to an assumption chip if it's wrong; Cuso updates the strategy.
6. Reads in "How we know it works" what Cuso tracks at each step; one line says Google Analytics, Search Console or Shopify are connected after approval (on Launch, and on Results or Channels in the app). The strategy itself has no connect buttons.
7. Approves. A strategy can always be approved: Cuso gets short turns to fill what is missing (six on cmo-v14 and later). Each turn names the two shortest weeks, what every network still lacks in them and the titles they already hold, so Cuso never needs the whole calendar. Only when those turns are spent does Core fit the strategy to what its calendar holds: every network's cadence (pictures and Reels apart) and its funnel plays, and a "Less than Cuso planned" card lists each cut. From cmo-v22 there are no finish turns and nothing is cut: the strategy is the plan and its examples, and Core plans the month after payment ([Making the month](../agent-flows/making-the-month.md)). Once approved, the step still offers a quiet "Write a new strategy" button until the first plan is bought, and its confirm step says what happens: the approved one goes back to a draft (free hiring again, ADR 0068), Cuso writes the new one, and approving it cancels the old one's unsent posts.

**They expect**

- A month they could picture: where, about what, which posts are videos; how many is a plan question, answered on Subscribe.
- The strategy ready in a few minutes, not half an hour (cmo-v22: the plan and a few example posts; the month is planned after payment).
- The month they approve holds as many posts as its own pace and channel cadences say, and every video its funnel promises.
- Changes reflected without starting over.
- A "No" or a correction reaches the whole strategy: the goal, the funnel, the questions, the ads line and every post title that rested on it, and the Brand Brain's summary, audience and offers say the corrected thing too. Nothing still names what they said no to (cmo-v8).
- Each network gets what suits it (cmo-v14): several X posts a day, at most one a day on LinkedIn, mostly pictures, TikToks, Reels and YouTube Shorts every day, every format by its platform's name; each funnel step says in plain words what happens for the business (Awareness: people find out about you; Interest: they look at what you offer; Desire: they want it; Action: they sign up, buy or book); every network helps at every step, leaning toward what it does best (cmo-v20 tags each post with its step); a connected network is never left out.
- Helpful tips about their buyers' world, not product tutorials in disguise, and every fact about the market with its source (cmo-v21).
- Every post makes its own point: no two posts in a week argue the same thing, and no point comes back more than three times in the month (cmo-v8).
- To understand what they will have to do themselves.
- To understand every line without being a marketer: plain words, no jargon (impressions, leads, funnel, cadence), and the same numbers on every screen (cmo-v15).
- No ads and no ad budget unless they said yes; the one question about ads is its Yes or No.
- To see what Cuso does for their website (the weekly audit, page fixes, articles when they ask), though it is not on the calendar, and to find it in the funnel.
- Questions that check an assumption with a yes or no, never whether they can count or share something.
- Posts planned everywhere their buyers could be, not only where they already have an account; the ones for a network they have not connected wait finished on the Calendar.
- No connect buttons for measurement on the strategy; those come after approval.

**Missing today:** TikTok is planned only once it is open for posting. A Cuso hired before cmo-v5 still plans only the networks the business has an account on.

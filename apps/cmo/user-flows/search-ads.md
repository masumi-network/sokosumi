# Search ads

**Who:** a business with a product people already search for, or a well-known competitor to take buyers from.

**Steps**

1. Sets a monthly ad budget on the strategy and connects Google Ads.
2. Asks Cuso for search ads, or presses "Ask Cuso for search ads" on the Competitors tab.
3. Cuso picks the searches that fit the budget ("<competitor> alternative", "<x> vs <y>", the product's buying searches) and writes one ad per search, with the search in the headline. Each ad comes with the text for its landing page.
4. The owner reads the ads on the Calendar and presses Launch.
5. Each week reads spend, clicks and cost per lead; agrees when Cuso suggests pausing a weak search.

**They expect**

- Cuso never spends money on his own; Launch is theirs.
- Spend never goes past the budget they set.
- Plain numbers: what it cost, what it brought.

**Today:** Google search ads only, phrase match. Meta campaigns are not made from CMO, and leads from their CRM don't flow back to Google yet.

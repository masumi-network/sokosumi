# Setup: Launch

**Steps**

1. Connects at least one network the strategy posts to; each row shows its posts.
2. Optionally connects Google Ads or Meta Ads, Google Analytics, Search Console, Shopify.
3. Presses Launch and lands in the app on Today.

**They expect**

- Each connection returns to CMO, in one go.
- Launch is clear about what's still missing and why.
- Right after Launch: the month starts being made, the first competitor check and SEO audit start.

# Setup: Subscribe

**Steps**

1. Sees what the approved month holds (posts, channels, pictures, videos; search ads apart from the posts, live only when they launch them) and the plans, monthly or yearly, each saying how many posts, pictures and videos it makes of this strategy a month and how many on each network ("X 84 · LinkedIn 28"); the smallest plan that makes all of it is picked.
2. Can read the CMO Terms, Privacy Policy, DPA and Imprint before paying. Pays through the hosted checkout.
3. Comes back to CMO and moves on to Launch.

**They expect**

- The price matches what they get; no surprise charges.
- A bigger plan makes more of the same month, right after they upgrade.
- Coming back from checkout lands exactly where they left.

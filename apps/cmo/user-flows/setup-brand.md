# Setup: Brand

**Steps**

1. Reads the Brand Brain: promise, customers, voice, look (logo, colours, fonts), the market map and competitors.
2. Opens "What I found on your site": every page of their site Cuso read (up to 50, one line each on what it says; a page opens to its facts and how it looks on a computer and a phone), each key page's sections (pricing, testimonials, product shots) and its pictures. While Cuso is still reading, it says so.
3. Corrects what's wrong, by editing a section or typing it. A correction made while Cuso writes the strategy follows it: CMO says so, and that it usually takes up to 15 minutes.
4. Presses "Looks like us".

**They expect**

- It's recognisably their business, with sources for what Cuso found.
- The pages and pictures are their own site's, each page linking to it, with no legal, sign-in or translated copies among them.
- The facts under a page are what that page says, prices and names exactly.
- A correction lands in the right section and sticks, and the Brand Brain's summary, audience and offers that said the same thing change with it (cmo-v8).
- A promise they rewrite reads as theirs, without the site named as its source.
- Competitors are real and relevant.

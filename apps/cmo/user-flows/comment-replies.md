# Comment replies

**Who:** a brand whose posts get comments it can't keep up with.

**Steps**

1. Does nothing: once Instagram, Facebook or YouTube is connected, Cuso goes through new comments every day.
2. Cuso answers the easy ones (thanks, simple questions) in the brand's voice, up to 20 a day.
3. Reads the daily note in chat: what he answered, and the comments he left for the owner (a complaint, a price question, a possible customer).
4. Answers those himself.

**They expect**

- No reply that could embarrass them; when in doubt, Cuso leaves it to them.
- To see what was said on their behalf.
- Leads and complaints never get a canned answer.

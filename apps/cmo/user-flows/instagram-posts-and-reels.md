# Instagram posts and Reels

**Who:** a brand with a product to show: a shop, an app, a service with a face.

**Steps**

1. Connects an Instagram business account (and the Facebook page, and YouTube if they have one). A Facebook login that manages several Pages asks "Which account?" and Cuso posts to the Page picked; Change in Channels picks another. A login with no Page, or a Google sign-in with no YouTube channel, says so instead of a general failure. A YouTube sign-in is one channel: to use another, connect again and pick that channel's account on Google's screen.
2. Sees the month's picture posts and carousels on the Calendar, made from the brand's own pictures.
3. Gets a short video each week: a presenter or product video with captions, music and B-roll. The same video goes out as an Instagram Reel, a Facebook Reel and a YouTube Short.
4. Watches the video in chat or on the Calendar and approves it, or asks for another take ("less formal", "show the app more").

**They expect**

- Pictures that look like their brand, not stock or obvious AI.
- The video plays in CMO before it goes anywhere.
- One video, every network, each with its own caption; they never upload anything twice.
- To know what a video costs before Cuso makes one.

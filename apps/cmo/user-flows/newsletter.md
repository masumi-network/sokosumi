# Newsletter

**Who:** a business with an email list it rarely writes to.

**Steps**

1. Connects or imports its mailing list.
2. Gets a draft issue each month (or week), made from the period's best posts, news and offers.
3. Reads it in chat, asks for changes ("lead with the launch").
4. Approves it and it goes out; later reads opens and clicks.

**They expect**

- One approval, then it's sent; no second tool.
- It sounds like the brand and links to their site.
- Open and click numbers in the month review.

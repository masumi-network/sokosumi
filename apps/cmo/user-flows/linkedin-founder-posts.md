# LinkedIn posts

**Who:** a founder who knows LinkedIn brings customers but never finds the time to post, as themselves or as their company Page.

**Steps**

1. Connects LinkedIn on Launch or in Channels. A sign-in that administers a Page asks "Which account?": the Pages first, "Your personal profile" last, and Cuso posts as the one picked. A sign-in with no Page connects the profile.
2. Checks Channels: a profile says "your personal profile" under LinkedIn, and Change signs in again to put a Page in its place. Posts that wait move to the new account.
3. Opens the Calendar and skims the week's two or three posts: stories, lessons, launches, in their own voice.
4. Changes one in chat: "shorter, less salesy", or "add that we just signed Acme". Or opens it, presses Edit text and fixes the words themselves.
5. Lets the rest go out on schedule.
6. On Friday reads Cuso's note on what the week's posts did.

**They expect**

- To choose who the posts are from. A company's Cuso never posts as the founder by accident: the connected account's name shows in Channels.
- Posts that sound like them, not like an AI: no hashtag walls, no emojis in every line, the link in the first comment.
- To change a post in one sentence, without opening an editor, and to fix a word themselves when that is quicker.
- A post written for a network that is not connected says so ("Waiting for LinkedIn") with Connect beside it, never just "Written".
- An opened post looks like it will on LinkedIn: their name, the words, the picture.
- Nothing goes out that they didn't have the chance to see on the Calendar.
- Real numbers (impressions, reactions) and which post worked best.

**Missing today**

- Numbers for a personal profile connected before Pages opened (through Composio): LinkedIn gives none for it. Change it in Channels to get them.
- Reading and answering comments.

# LinkedIn founder posts

**Who:** a founder who knows LinkedIn brings customers but never finds the time to post.

**Steps**

1. Connects their LinkedIn profile on Launch or in Channels. A company page cannot be connected yet: every post goes out as the person.
2. Opens the Calendar and skims the week's two or three posts: stories, lessons, launches, in their own voice.
3. Changes one in chat: "shorter, less salesy", or "add that we just signed Acme". Or opens it, presses Edit text and fixes the words themselves.
4. Lets the rest go out on schedule.
5. On Friday reads Cuso's note on what the week's posts did.

**They expect**

- Posts that sound like them, not like an AI: no hashtag walls, no emojis in every line, the link in the first comment.
- To change a post in one sentence, without opening an editor, and to fix a word themselves when that is quicker.
- A post written for a network that is not connected says so ("Waiting for LinkedIn") with Connect beside it, never just "Written".
- An opened post looks like it will on LinkedIn: their name, the words, the picture.
- Nothing goes out that they didn't have the chance to see on the Calendar.
- Real numbers (impressions, reactions) and which post worked best.

# Reacting to news

**Who:** a founder in a market that moves: competitors, laws, big launches.

**Steps**

1. Does nothing: Cuso reads the industry's news every day and the competitors' sites, news and ads every week.
2. When something matters (a competitor drops its price, a new rule, a big launch), Cuso writes in chat: what happened, with the source, what it means for them, and a post or a move.
3. The owner approves the post, or presses "Add to strategy" on the move.
4. Looks at the Competitors tab now and then for the week's moves.

**They expect**

- Only news that matters to their business, with the source to check it.
- No made-up claims about competitors.
- A reaction while the news is still fresh: the same day.

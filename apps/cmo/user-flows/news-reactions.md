# Reacting to news

**Who:** a founder in a market that moves: competitors, laws, big launches.

**Steps**

1. Does nothing: Cuso reads the industry's news every day and the competitors' sites, news and ads every week.
2. When something in the industry matters (a new rule, a big launch), Cuso writes in chat: what happened, with the source, what it means for them, and a post or a move.
3. After each weekly competitor check, reads one card in chat, "What your competitors did this week": one line per move with its source. Only a big move (a price change on the competitor's own pricing page; a launch, funding or an acquisition the competitor or a newsroom reported) also says what it means for them and what to do.
4. Presses "See every move" for the Competitors tab, where each move has what it means, a suggested move and "Add to strategy".
5. Approves a post Cuso drafted, or presses "Add to strategy" on a move.

**They expect**

- Only news that matters to their business, with the source to check it.
- No made-up claims about competitors.
- A reaction while the news is still fresh: the same day.
- One message per competitor check, never one per move, and nothing called big that only a tracker, a directory or a blog reports.

# Setup: hire and research

**Who:** a founder who just heard of CMO.XYZ.

**Steps**

1. Taps "Try Cuso free" on www.cmo.xyz (or opens app.cmo.xyz, which goes straight to Log in), signs up with an email code and enters their website.
2. Picks where Cuso goes (a new organization by default).
3. Watches Cuso read the site: steps tick off, the homepage screenshots and first findings fill in.
4. Answers his yes/no questions one at a time while he reads, adding details where needed.
5. From cmo-v21, once the Brand Brain is saved, Core researches the buyers' industry (about five web searches: news, trends, common problems, competitors) before Cuso writes the strategy; it lands in Files under Brand research/Industry.

**They expect**

- To see work happening within seconds, and a finished read in a few minutes. Recent progress keeps the research running even when the server clocks differ.
- Questions that make sense for their business, never about things the site already says.
- No Sokosumi name anywhere; nothing to pay yet.

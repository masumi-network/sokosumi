# TikTok

**Cuso does**

- Plans TikTok videos in the month's strategy once the owner wants the channel: talking videos and short videos made from brand pictures. From cmo-v14: every short video of the month, the same file as its Reel and YouTube Short: seven a day at the top plan (Starter about two a day, 17 a week; Growth four a day), most of them five-second TikToks, at most four talking ones a month. From cmo-v16 those short videos are shorts without a person on camera, 15 to 30 seconds each: a story with a voice, a tour of the brand's website, a numbered top 3 list or a photo slideshow, varied across the month. Cuso writes each one's script and the words on screen at the start; Core cuts it from a scene picture, the brand's own pictures and a live capture of its website, with captions, music and the logo at the end (about 14 credits, against 21 for a five-second one).
- Writes each one for TikTok: hook in the first second, captions in the safe area, AI label on.
- Hands each finished video to the owner. He never posts to TikTok himself, and never says a video was posted or scheduled.

**The owner should see:** each finished video waiting for them in the Calendar, with its caption, who it posts as and the confirmation TikTok asks for; then live on TikTok, marked as AI-made.

**Missing today:** a posting time (a video goes out when the owner posts it), reading and answering TikTok comments, and TikTok numbers in the weekly review.

# Google Ads

**Cuso does**

1. Proposes search ads on buying searches and on competitor searches ("X alternative", "X vs Y"), phrase match, inside the strategy's budget; each with the text for its landing page.
2. Waits for the owner to press Launch. Never starts or raises spend himself; Core holds the budget cap.
3. Weekly after launch: reads spend, clicks and cost per lead, pauses weak searches, adds negative keywords, proposes budget shifts for the owner to approve.

**The owner should see:** ads on the Calendar with Launch; a weekly line with spend and what it brought.

**Missing today:** step 3 (he reports, he doesn't tune yet); leads from the owner's CRM don't flow back to Google.

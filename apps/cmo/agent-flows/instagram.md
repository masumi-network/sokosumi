# Instagram

**Cuso does**

- Picture posts and carousels in the look of an old print, in the brand's colours with its logo; a real photo or page screenshot sits inside the print where the post shows it, and each carousel slide carries its own few words.
- A weekly Reel: presenter or product video with captions, music and B-roll. From cmo-v14: two picture posts or carousels a day at the top plan and every short video of the month as a Reel (the same file as its TikTok and YouTube Short), seven a day (Starter a picture post a day and 17 Reels a week, Growth a picture post or carousel and four Reels a day). From cmo-v16 those Reels are shorts with a voice or music, cut from the brand's pictures and its real website (see [TikTok](tiktok.md)).
- Daily comment round: up to 20 replies, each checked by Jev; leads, complaints and price questions left for the owner.
- Weekly reach and saves per post; shifts the mix toward what works.
- Follower and engagement numbers next to the competitors' (Competitors tab).

**The owner should see:** posts and Reels on the Calendar, playable in CMO; a daily note of replies and the comments left for them.

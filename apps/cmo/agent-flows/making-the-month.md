# Making the month

**When:** right after the owner approves the strategy and pays, and again for each new month.

**Cuso does**

1. Writes the pieces of the calendar the bought plan makes (Starter, Growth or Scale; all of it on Tester credits), in the strategy's mix and pillars, in the audience's language.
2. Picks an old print from the reference library for every picture, a different one per piece, and the few words it carries; a carousel gets one print for all its slides and words per slide. Where a picture must show the real site or product, he names that brand picture (a page screenshot, a section of a page, a photo).
3. Core makes each picture in that print's look with the brand's colours and logo, sets the named brand picture inside it unchanged, and checks it (words spelled right, nothing copied from the print, nothing invented on the brand's page). A picture that fails is made again, on a stronger model from the third try; one that fails every try comes back to him for a new visual and print. When he changes a piece's words or visual after its picture is on the post, Core takes the old picture off, makes it again and schedules the post at its time once the new one is on. Videos are made from the brand's own pictures; a piece that names none starts at another of them.
4. Schedules each post on its connected network. A piece for a network that isn't connected is made all the same (words, picture or video) and waits finished; it is scheduled once the owner connects the account. From cmo-v5 on the month holds every network CMO offers, so most owners have such pieces.
5. Jev reads the month against the writing skills and the brand voice; one rewrite run fixes what misses.

**The owner should see:** one progress card in chat, then a full Calendar where every made piece opens; nothing goes out that isn't on it.

**Missing today:** a way for the owner to turn the old-print look off, or to pick the prints.

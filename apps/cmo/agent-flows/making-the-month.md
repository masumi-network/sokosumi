# Making the month

**When:** right after the owner approves the strategy and pays, and again for each new month.

**Cuso does**

1. Writes the pieces of the calendar the bought plan makes (Starter, Growth or Scale; all of it on Tester credits), in the strategy's mix and pillars, in the audience's language. From cmo-v8 each calendar entry carries the one claim it argues: the strategy review names a claim argued twice in a week or more than three times in the month, and once the month is in Core gives the later pieces of a repeated claim their own, and moves every piece past the third on one point (across all networks) onto a point of its own: a new title, brief and claim from the Brand Brain's facts, before the owner approves. A story is asked only of a social-proof piece. From cmo-v20 Core first gives every piece still without a claim its point, in a call of its own before any words are written: it lists the Brand Brain's facts once each and holds one fact to about one piece in sixteen on a network (X at 99 posts: 7), so no single point leads the month. It also marks the offer pieces the funnel promises, two a week on each network of its action stage: each ends with the offer's action, with its link in the first comment on X, LinkedIn and Facebook or "link in bio" on Instagram and TikTok, and Core sends one back without it. Each chunk of the writer reads only its own network's writing skill.
2. Picks an old print from the reference library for every picture, a different one per piece, and the few words it carries; a carousel gets one print for all its slides and words per slide. From cmo-v8 the prints vary in kind: an infographic only for a picture that shows numbers or a comparison, at most one picture in four, and neighbouring pieces on one network never in the same kind (Core picks within these, and refuses a save that breaks them). Where a picture must show the real site or product, he names that brand picture (a page screenshot, a section of a page, a photo).
3. Core makes each picture in that print's look with the brand's colours and logo, places the named brand picture itself (unchanged, in a browser frame on a panel the picture leaves free; the model never redraws it). A named page shows the way the format needs it: on a phone in a short, reel or story, on a computer or as one of its sections in a feed picture, another section per piece so a month does not repeat one shot. From cmo-v20 he finds any page of the site, its facts and its screenshots in sitePages and the page's note. Core checks each picture (words spelled right, nothing copied from the print, nothing invented on the brand's page). A picture that fails is made again, on a stronger model from the third try; one that fails every try comes back to him for a new visual and print. When he changes a piece's words or visual after its picture is on the post, Core takes the old picture off, makes it again and schedules the post at its time once the new one is on. Videos are made from the brand's own pictures; a piece that names none starts at another of them.
4. Schedules each post on its connected network. A piece for a network that isn't connected is made all the same (words, picture or video) and waits finished; it is scheduled once the owner connects the account. From cmo-v5 on the month holds every network CMO offers, so most owners have such pieces.
5. Jev reads the month against the writing skills and the brand voice; one rewrite run fixes what misses, including a piece whose words argue another piece's claim. The review records how many pictures took each kind of print.

**The owner should see:** one progress card in chat, then a full Calendar where every made piece opens; nothing goes out that isn't on it.

**Missing today:** a way for the owner to turn the old-print look off, or to pick the prints.

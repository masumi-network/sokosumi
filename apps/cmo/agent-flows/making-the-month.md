# Making the month

**When:** right after the owner approves the strategy and pays, and again for each new month.

**Cuso does**

1. Writes every piece on the calendar, in the strategy's mix and pillars, in the audience's language.
2. Picks an old print from the reference library for every picture, a different one per piece, and the few words it carries; a carousel gets one print for all its slides and words per slide. Where a picture must show the real site or product, he names that brand picture.
3. Core makes each picture in that print's look with the brand's colours and logo, sets the named brand picture inside it unchanged, and checks it (words spelled right, nothing copied from the print, nothing invented on the brand's page). A picture that fails is made again, on a stronger model from the third try; one that fails every try comes back to him for a new visual and print. Videos are made from the brand's own pictures.
4. Schedules each post on its connected network; a piece for a network that isn't connected waits.
5. Jev reads the month against the writing skills and the brand voice; one rewrite run fixes what misses.

**The owner should see:** one progress card in chat, then a full Calendar where every made piece opens; nothing goes out that isn't on it.

**Missing today:** a way for the owner to turn the old-print look off, or to pick the prints.

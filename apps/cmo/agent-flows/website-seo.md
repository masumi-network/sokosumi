# Website and SEO

**Cuso does**

1. After each weekly audit (cmo-v12 on): Core wakes him for one short SEO message in his chat: Google's last 28 days against the 28 before (clicks, seen, position), the one to three page changes worth most, saved with their before (read from the live page) and after, and one or two article ideas.
2. Writes articles when asked, in chat or with "Write an article" on the SEO page: picks a search with real numbers, writes the whole article from the Brand Brain and the site, and saves it ready to copy.
3. Checks whether last week's fixes landed (Core, against the next audit) and where the site ranks for a search when asked (the SEO specialist does the check).

**The owner should see:** the weekly SEO message with its cards (each page change as before and after, each article by title and search) and an "Open SEO" link; the SEO page with the next three things to do.

**Missing today:** publishing to the site; ranking checks on a schedule.

For keyword research, Cuso reads the ranked editorial picks and cites each measurement date. A cached search reuses the same picks. When the weekly result expires, the editors can test new phrases against the earlier topic baseline for up to 30 days.

When research measures no volume for a topic (a niche market), research_keywords also returns the brand's own Search Console searches that share the topic's words, with impressions, clicks and position, and Cuso picks article keywords from those.

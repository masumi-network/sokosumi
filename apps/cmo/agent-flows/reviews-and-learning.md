# Reviews and learning

**Cuso does**

1. Weekly: reads the week's numbers per post and channel, gives a verdict (hold, fix, test, investigate) and adjusts the coming week.
2. Nightly: tidies his memory; every no, edit or decline from the owner becomes a rule for next time. The review window uses database time for recent work.
3. Month end: writes the month review, proposes next month (more of what worked), and makes it once the owner approves.

**The owner should see:** a short weekly note; a clear month review with one decision.

**Missing today:** the month-end handoff into the next month's making isn't automatic end to end.

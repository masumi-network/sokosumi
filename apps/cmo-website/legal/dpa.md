## Processing details

Subject matter: AI marketing services for the customer's websites and connected business accounts, provided on the Sokosumi platform.

Duration: the service term plus the period needed to return or delete data under this DPA, subject to lawful retention.

Purpose and operations: receiving and storing instructions and files; researching public websites; building brand records and strategies; generating and reviewing text, images, audio and video; reading authorized account data; scheduling and publishing content; replying to supported comments; producing analytics and reports; providing support and securing those operations.

Data subjects: the customer's staff and authorized users; customers, prospects and social followers; people whose comments or public business information enter the service; and people depicted in authorized media.

Data categories: identifiers and contact details; professional and business information; public profile and comment information; account identifiers and integration credentials; marketing content, images, audio and video; instructions and messages; connected analytics, advertising and commerce information; and related usage and technical records.

Sensitive data: the service is not intended for special-category data, criminal-offence records or children's data. The customer must not submit it unless the parties first agree in writing on the need and appropriate safeguards. Images and voices can identify people; the customer must have a lawful basis and the necessary permissions for their use.

The customer determines which websites, data sources and accounts to connect, which content to provide and the instructions and authorizations for the work. Processing is ongoing as needed to provide those functions. No sale of customer personal data is authorized by this DPA.

## Instructions and confidentiality

Sumico will process customer personal data only on documented instructions, including these terms, the customer's approved settings and strategies, and authorized service requests. This includes instructions about transfers. If law requires processing beyond those instructions, Sumico will inform the customer before processing unless that law prohibits notice on important public-interest grounds.

Sumico will promptly inform the customer if it believes an instruction infringes applicable data protection law and may pause the affected processing while the parties resolve it. The customer remains responsible for lawful collection, required notices, rights and permissions, and the instructions it gives.

People authorized to process customer personal data must be bound by confidentiality duties or an appropriate statutory duty. Access must be limited to those who need it for the service, support, security or legal obligations.

## Security and incidents

Sumico will implement technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk, taking account of the state of the art, implementation costs and the nature and purposes of processing. The agreed measures are recorded in the Security Annex below. Measures must include suitable access management, protection of credentials and transfers, incident handling and regular assessment of their effectiveness. Changes must not materially reduce the agreed protection.

Sumico will notify the customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting customer personal data. It will provide available information about the nature of the breach, affected data and people, likely consequences, measures taken or proposed, and a contact for follow-up. Information may be supplied in phases without undue further delay.

Sumico will take reasonable steps to contain and remedy the breach and assist the customer with its notification duties. The customer decides whether to notify authorities and individuals unless law requires Sumico to act directly. A breach notice is not an admission of liability.

Services the customer independently selects as controllers, such as its social platforms or its merchant-of-record payment service, are not automatically Sumico subprocessors. Their independent controller roles are described in the Privacy Policy.

## Assistance and accountability

Taking account of the nature of processing, Sumico will assist the customer with requests to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to or port customer personal data. If a person contacts Sumico directly about customer-controlled data, Sumico will promptly pass the request to the customer and will not answer on its behalf unless instructed or legally required.

Taking account of the information available to it, Sumico will assist with security obligations, breach notifications, data protection impact assessments and prior consultation with supervisory authorities. The parties will cooperate with lawful regulatory requests.

Sumico will make available information needed to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and allow and contribute to audits, including inspections, by the customer or an independent auditor it appoints. Routine audits require reasonable notice, confidentiality and reasonable steps to protect other customers and service security. Those arrangements must not prevent an audit required by law, a regulator or a relevant incident. Parties will agree reasonable costs for extraordinary assistance in advance; costs must not obstruct statutory rights or correction of Sumico's own breach.

## International transfers

The Transfer Annex incorporates the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK Addendum, with Swiss adaptations where relevant. Sumico does not rely on its own Data Privacy Framework certification. A valid adequacy decision may be used for a covered recipient and transfer. Sumico will assess transfer risks, apply necessary supplementary measures and suspend or avoid a transfer if the required protection cannot be provided.

## Return and deletion

At the customer's choice, Sumico will return or delete customer personal data after the service ends, and delete existing copies unless law requires retention. The agreed export method, active-system deletion deadline and backup deletion cycle are: return through the available download functions or an agreed commonly used electronic format on request; deletion from active systems within 30 days after the end of the service or the customer's deletion instruction, and from backups within 90 days after active deletion. A customer instruction to keep an archive extends storage only for that instructed purpose. Data legally required to be retained is isolated and used only for that obligation.

Until deletion is complete, retained data remains protected under this DPA and may be processed only for the agreed wind-down, security or legally required purpose. On request, Sumico will confirm completion, subject to any identified legal retention duty. The customer must separately manage published data and independently connected platforms. Start over is not itself an instruction to delete all account and service records.

## Security Annex

The following measures apply to customer personal data according to the risk and the processing involved:

- Access control: authenticated, workspace-scoped access; least-privilege permissions for personnel and providers; removal of access when no longer needed; confidentiality duties and appropriate staff instruction.
- Credentials and transmission: encrypted CMO session and OAuth token cookies; secure transport for production service connections; restricted access to integration credentials and keys; rotation or revocation when compromised.
- Storage and isolation: logical separation of customer workspaces, access-controlled storage and encryption at rest using the relevant infrastructure provider's facilities. Publicly published media is intentionally accessible to its selected audience and must not contain confidential data.
- Reliability and recovery: backups and recovery procedures appropriate to the system; controlled changes; monitoring of availability and errors; periodic assessment of recovery and security measures.
- Incident management: documented escalation, investigation, containment and customer notification; preservation of relevant evidence with restricted access.
- Data minimization and deletion: limit data sent to AI and integration providers to the task; apply the retention and deletion schedule; restrict restored backup data and reapply deletion instructions.

Sumico will regularly assess these measures and provide reasonable compliance information under the audit clause. Customer controls include account permissions, connection grants, strategy approval and publication review. The customer must maintain secure access to its own accounts and devices.
